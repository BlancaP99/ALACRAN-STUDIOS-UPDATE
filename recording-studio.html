Recording Studio.

A world-class recording environment where pristine sound, carefully selected equipment and an intimate creative atmosphere come together in the heart of Miami Beach.

Built for Exceptional Sound.

The studio is designed around warmth, precision and creative focus. At the center of the control room is an SSL Duality Delta SuperAnalogue console, supported by a finely tuned monitoring system and a complete analog and digital workflow.

The Live Room is known for its tight, punchy acoustic signature and features a large window overlooking Miami Beach, allowing natural light into the space. Combined with a top-grade microphone collection, vintage outboard equipment and studio-ready instruments, the room supports everything from intimate vocal sessions to full-band productions.
