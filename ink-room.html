The Ink Room.

A flexible multimedia space for live streaming, podcasting, video production, rehearsals, writing sessions and live-band recording in the heart of Miami Beach.

Built for Modern Content.

The Ink Room is a highly modular multimedia space designed for contemporary content creation. A dedicated high-speed fiber optic network supports reliable live streaming, remote production and broadcast-ready workflows.

Professional audio, video and lighting systems make the room suitable for live-band recordings, rehearsals, writing sessions, podcasts, press junkets, interview tapings, castings and conference meetings. Analog and digital lines connect the Ink Room directly with the Recording Studio, expanding the creative possibilities across both spaces.
