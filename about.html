Where Sound Meets Creativity.

Located in the heart of Miami's South Beach, Alacran Studios is a world-class recording and multimedia facility created to inspire artists, producers and creative teams.

Built to Bring Visions to Life.

Just steps from Ocean Drive, two intrepid women set out to create a studio where cutting-edge technology and artistic elegance could exist in perfect balance. The result is an intimate, beautifully designed sanctuary dedicated entirely to creativity.

A meticulously curated collection of modern and vintage instruments, microphones and recording equipment supports a complete creative workflow, while preserving the warmth, precision and character that discerning musicians expect.

For clients seeking the utmost discretion, a private garage provides secure, direct access into the 5,000 sq ft facility—an immersive, purpose-built environment where pristine sound meets inspired creativity.
