Built for Focus and Scale.

Located in the heart of Miami Beach, Alacran Studios combines the privacy of an intimate creative environment with the technical capacity of a high-end production facility.

The studio is designed to support the complete creative process: writing, tracking, podcasting, mixing, mastering, streaming, sound design and audiovisual post-production. Private access and a carefully considered layout allow teams to work comfortably, discreetly and without interruption.

Control Room · Live Room

Recording Studio.

A world-class recording studio built around exceptional audio quality in a warm, intimate and inspiring environment. The control room is centered on an SSL Duality Delta SuperAnalogue console and a finely tuned monitoring system.

The Live Room is known for its tight, punchy acoustic signature and features a large window overlooking Miami Beach, allowing natural light into the space. Combined with an extensive microphone collection and vintage outboard equipment, the studio supports everything from intimate vocal sessions to full-band and audiovisual productions.

The Ink Room.

A highly flexible multimedia room designed for modern content creation. The space is hard-wired to a dedicated high-speed fiber optic network and equipped with professional audio, video, lighting and streaming systems.

The Ink Room connects directly to the Recording Studio through analog and digital lines, opening up reliable workflows for live streaming, live-band recording, rehearsals, writing sessions, podcasts, press junkets, interviews, castings and conference meetings.
